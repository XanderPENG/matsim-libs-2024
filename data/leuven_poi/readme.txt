POI (Points of Interest) dataset for your GIS  
=========================================================  

## About  

Get POI data in a convenient format using data.nextgis.com  

Source:  
   * OpenStreetMap (https://osm.org) – database of open spatial data and a community of mappers who keep it up-to-date.  
   * Overture Maps Foundation (https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/) – collaborative open map dataset which contains more than 64 million point representations of real-world entities: schools, businesses, hospitals, religious organizations, landmarks, mountain peaks, and much more..  
   * Foursquare (https://opensource.foursquare.com/os-places/) – Open Source Places dataset which offers over 100 million global Points of Interest (POIs) across 1,000+ categories in more than 200 countries and territories.   


## Contents   

Each dataset contains the following data layers:   
    * Overture POI (points) (poi-overture-point)   
    * Foursquare POI (points) (poi-fsq-point)   
    * OpenStreetMap POI (points) (poi-osm-point)  
    * OpenStreetMap POI (polygons) (poi-osm-polygon)
